# Pokémon Revelation

Based off RHH's [pokeemerald-expansion](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/) 1.11.2

## What features are included? (WIP)

`*` Feature in development.

- **Latest battle mechanics**
    - Mega Evolutions!
    - No Z-Moves, Dynamax or Terastallization!
    - Hidden Abilities!
    - Moves, Abilities, etc upto modern standards!
    - All hold items!
    - Scarlet Violet TMs + a few more!

- **Competitive focus**
    - No grinding needed!
    - All level 100!
    - Customizable Moves, EVs and IVS!
    - Changeable abilities and natures!
    - Buyable Pokémon with complete sets! `*`
    - Dynamic move type, power and accuracy! `*`

- **Story features:**
    - There is NO story…
    - Regional dex of 404 Pokémon from Generations 1-9!
    - Custom Gym Leaders!
    - A new Pokémon League gauntlet tournament called Revelation Tournament with 6 battles!
    - More below.

### **Battles:**
    - Losing to ANY Trainer resets the whole challenge and you'll be sent to the lobby again.

#### **Gym Battles:**
    - Each Gym contains 3 Trainers.
    - Gym Trainer and Leader specialize in the same type.
        1. Gym Trainer - Weaker than Gym Leader
        2. Gym Leader - Stronger than Gym Trainer
        3. Post Gym Trainer - Specializes in a different type
    - You can fight Gym Leaders in any order, but the order for a Gym will be 1, 2 inside the Gym and 3 right outside.

#### **Challengers' Arena:**
    - After beating the 8 Gyms, there will be 6 Challengers and a Champion!
    - This will be a gauntlet - meaning after one battle, you will straight away battle another til all 6 Challengers are defeated.
    - After the 6 Challengers are defeated, there will be a final battle against the Champion.
